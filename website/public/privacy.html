Social Space Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

Social Space is a desktop app that Soft Wings uses to schedule and publish its own videos to its own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X). It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When an account owner connects a social account, the app receives the access the owner approves: basic profile information (such as account ID, display name and avatar) and permission to upload or publish videos to that account.

How we use it

The information is used only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos and captions the owner chooses. We do not read, collect or analyze other users' data, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer in Windows Credential Manager. Videos awaiting partner approval are stored in our private Firebase project and removed when no longer needed.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account in the app at any time, or revoke access from the social platform's own settings. Tokens are deleted from the computer when an account is disconnected.
